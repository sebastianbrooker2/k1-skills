Rondo: win the ball

Three robots pass the ball around and keep it away from you. They don't know where you are: each one only sees you through its own camera, and they share their detections in one tracker (the orange ghost).

Objective: get the ball. Touch it to win, or press them into losing it out of the rondo before they make 8 passes. See how fast you can do it.

Watch their cameras on the side: when they lose sight of you (behind one of them, or out of their view) their guess drifts, they misjudge their passes, and you're far more likely to get to the ball.
